Illustrative scenario, not a named person

"Next winter, do I plant wheat on wheat again, or put in a lupin or canola break crop?"

This page shows what the sources say, where they disagree and what is missing. It cannot tell you which choice will pay on your field.

Plain answer

No winner can be named from today's sources

Under the sources we have, we cannot name a winner between wheat on wheat and a lupin or canola break crop.

Why

    What would settle it

    • A multi-season trial that counts the break crop's income, disease relief and nitrogen credit.
    • Field-level rain records, and a sourced water-use figure for the Overberg.
    • Measured soil, not a first-look map.

    How to read the badges on this page: checked a source was opened and read, unverified sourced, not opened by us or not verified for this site, assumed no source found.

    What would change the answer

    This does not change the plain answer above. It shows which way the evidence would lean under different conditions, using only figures already on this page. There is no score and no ranking. Each statement shows its inputs and arithmetic.
