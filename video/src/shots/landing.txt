The rotation analysis has four parts
DESCRIPTIVE
What the sources show
Rain from four sources, soil by depth, the one sourced trial.
DIAGNOSTIC
Why the answer is open
Where sources disagree, and which cells of the comparison have no source.
PREDICTIVE
What would change the answer
Conditional ranges computed from the ledger. No forecast, no score.
PRESCRIPTIVE
What to do next
Which document or measurement would settle each gap. Never a crop choice.

Today 0 of 8 cells of the rotation comparison have a sourced value, so Fallow names no winner. Every number in its text and charts is a row in one ledger, or computed from those rows, with its source, its status and what would raise it.
