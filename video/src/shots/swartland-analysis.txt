Swartland: wheat again, or a break crop?

Scope tier: Full profile (uncertainty example). The plate, the rotation analysis in four parts and the full ledger.

Plain answer

No winner can be named from today's sources

Under the sources we have, we cannot name a winner between wheat on wheat and a lupin or canola break crop for Swartland.

0 of 8 metric cells have a sourced value. Our ruleour threshold, not from a source: no winner, no score and no ranking colour while fewer than half are sourced.

Why
The one sourced trial (ARC-SGI) shows wheat yielding less after a break crop in 7 of 9 cells. It counts wheat yield only, not the break crop's income, disease relief or nitrogen credit.
Most of what would decide it has no source we could find: the break crop's water need, its nitrogen credit and root depth.
Under our 15% rule, Overberg rain sources agree: they are within about 11% (394 to 444 mm a year). In the Swartland they are 182 mm a year apart, so rain alone cannot settle a choice there.
